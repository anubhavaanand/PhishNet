Everything a phisher hopes you don't have.

02 — How it works

A transformer in your tab, not our cloud.

01 / EXTRACT

Read the email locally

A content script extracts subject, body, links and attachments from the open message in Gmail or Outlook Web.

02 / INFER

Classify on-device

The text runs through a quantized DistilBERT model via Transformers.js + ONNX Runtime Web inside an isolated offscreen document.

03 / MARK

Show the verdict

A badge is injected into the header. Suspicious links get highlighted. Click through for full forensic detail.

03 — Privacy

Your inbox never leaves home.

Most "email security" tools work by forwarding your mail to their servers. PhishNet flips the model: the ~50 MB model downloads once, then every scan happens on your silicon. We couldn't read your email if we wanted to.

  • ✓ Zero network requests during inference
  • ✓ No analytics, telemetry or tracking of any kind
  • ✓ Model weights cached locally after first load
  • ✓ History & whitelist stored only in browser storage
  • ✓ Fully open source — audit every line
  • ✓ Works offline once cached
